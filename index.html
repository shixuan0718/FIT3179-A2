This story focuses on the fashion products entering Malaysia, where they come from, when retail activity rises, and how the product mix and prices have changed over time. Household spending appears only as context — not as a measure of fashion preference.

01

THE MARKET AROUND THE WARDROBE

Where does Malaysia’s fashion market sit?

One map provides affordability context; the remaining maps are fashion-specific, showing current clothing price pressure and the international supply geography behind Malaysia’s apparel and footwear market.

Context only: total household expenditure is not fashion spending, but it shows where consumers have more or less room for discretionary purchases.

Fashion-specific snapshot: Jan–Aug 2026 clothing inflation ranges from about −3.1% in Labuan to +1.7% in Negeri Sembilan.

Supply chain: China and Viet Nam dominate the largest import routes; thicker lines mean higher 2025 apparel and footwear import value.

Concentration: China alone supplies about USD 1.18 billion of the three selected fashion chapters in 2025, far ahead of the next-largest origins.

02

PRODUCTS & SUPPLIERS

What fashion products enter Malaysia?

2025 import composition

Product mix: knitted apparel is the largest import category, while China appears as the biggest supplier block across all three product groups.

Supplier specialisation: Bangladesh is strongly knitted-apparel-heavy, while Viet Nam and Indonesia lean more toward footwear; China dominates overall volume.

Trade asymmetry: China dominates Malaysia’s import side, while the United States is the largest export destination among the major partners shown.

03

RHYTHMS & SHIFTS

When does fashion shopping lift — and how is the mix changing?

The retail series shows a clearer year-end lift than a Hari Raya spike. Alongside that seasonal rhythm, trade data shows how the mix of knitted apparel, woven apparel and footwear has shifted over the last decade.

December stands out: MSIC 477 sales average about 5.8% above each year’s mean in 2023–2025. Group 477 is a broad specialised-store proxy and includes more than fashion.

Price differences: the heatmap shows how clothing inflation varies by state and year, with 2026 representing the available months through August.

Changing mix: knitted apparel remains the largest import category, but the relative size of all three product groups changes over time. This is a market-availability signal, not a direct survey of individual preferences.

04

THE PRICE STORY

Clothing prices did not follow Malaysia’s wider inflation path

KEY TAKEAWAY

Across the 16 state and territory series, the clothing-and-footwear index averages about 90 in Aug 2026, while the all-items index averages about 134. In other words, clothing prices have fallen relative to 2010 while the broader basket became much more expensive.

How to read this visualisation

Who

Designed for the average Malaysian reader, with plain-language labels and no assumed statistical background.

What

OpenDOSM retail, CPI and household-context data combined with UN Comtrade HS 61, 62 and 64 apparel and footwear trade data.

Why

To compare fashion product categories, seasonal retail trends and changes over time, while showing the supply geography and price context around them.

How

Maps show market geography; a treemap and supplier-profile plot compare product categories; radial, heatmap, streamgraph and line charts reveal seasonal and long-term change.
