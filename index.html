Stitched Economy

Which fashion products arrive in Malaysia, who supplies them, and how the mix and retail seasons change.

Fashion shopping starts with products: knitted apparel, woven apparel and footwear. Discover who supplies them, how their import mix changed from 2015 to 2025, and when activity rises in fashion-related retail stores. State incomes and clothing prices provide context. These trade figures describe market supply, not a survey of individual shoppers. Hover over the charts to investigate the data.

Words you will meet

Price index (CPI)
A number that tracks what a typical basket of goods costs. If it moves from 100 to 110, prices rose 10%.
Imports and exports
Imports are goods Malaysia buys from other countries. Exports are goods it sells to them.
Knitted and woven apparel
Knitted means stretchy clothes such as T-shirts, jumpers and socks. Woven means shirts, trousers and jackets.
Fashion-related retail
MSIC group 477 includes shops selling clothing, footwear and other goods. Its monthly pattern is an indicator, not a precise total for clothes alone.

Fashion around the map

Where do Malaysia’s fashion products come from?

Import hotspots reveal which partners send Malaysia the most knitted apparel, woven apparel and footwear. Two Malaysian maps then put these products into context: clothing price changes and the different budgets households have available.

02

Fashion products and suppliers

Which types of clothes and footwear does Malaysia trade?

Compare product categories, the suppliers behind them, and the gap between imports and exports. These trade values describe Malaysia’s market supply, not individual shoppers’ preferences.

03

Rhythms and shifts

When does fashion retail rise, and what changes over time?

Retail activity varies across the months, while imported product categories evolve over the years. The retail series includes some non-fashion products, and the import mix tracks what enters the market rather than directly measuring personal preferences.

04

The price story

Is fashion getting more affordable?

Fashion-related retail, import categories and consumer budgets tell different parts of the story. One striking price context is that clothing and footwear prices fell while the wider cost of living rose.

What to take away
  1. Products: knitted apparel represents about 52% of the selected 2025 import bill, up from 47% in 2015.
  2. Suppliers: China accounts for about 36% of the selected imports, but supplier product mixes differ sharply.
  3. Retail: the broad MSIC 477 monthly pattern is modest, with December around 2.4% above a typical month.
  4. Prices: across the unweighted state series, clothing and footwear CPI has fallen relative to 2010, unlike the overall CPI. This is context, not evidence of preference.

About this visualisation

Who

Designed for the average Malaysian reader, with plain-language labels and no statistics knowledge assumed.

What

UN Comtrade HS 61 (knitted apparel), HS 62 (woven apparel) and HS 64 (footwear), combined with OpenDOSM retail and clothing CPI data and household-budget context, plus geographic boundaries.

Why

To compare apparel and footwear categories, show how the available product mix changes over time, and describe seasonal retail activity for Malaysian readers. Imports are a market-supply proxy, not a survey of individual preferences.

How

Four map idioms show supplier geography and price/budget context; a treemap and parallel coordinates compare fashion categories and suppliers; radial, heatmap, streamgraph and line charts reveal seasonal and long-term patterns.

Notes and limits

Household income is included as market context, not clothing spending. Group 477 shops also sell goods other than fashion. Import category values and supplier shares represent goods entering Malaysia, not observed customer choices or preferences. State price figures are unweighted averages of 16 states and territories, not an official national index. Seasonal figures use 2023–2025 with year-level growth removed. The ten top supplier map points cover 91% of 2025 imports in the three selected chapters; 2025 Comtrade values may be revised.
