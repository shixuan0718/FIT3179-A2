Topic 8: Fashion and Clothing. This visualisation explores clothing and fashion products using retail and trade datasets. It compares product categories and seasonal retail trends, then tracks how the market product mix changes over time. Because the available datasets do not measure individual preferences directly, changes in imports are presented as a market-level signal rather than a consumer survey.

01

WHERE FASHION COMES FROM

Which countries supply Malaysia’s clothes and footwear?

The opening maps are all fashion-specific: they show supplier specialisation, clothing-price pressure across Malaysian states, and the international routes and hotspots behind apparel and footwear imports.

Different suppliers, different products: the largest import origins do not have the same mix — some lean toward knitted apparel, others toward footwear or woven apparel.

Fashion-specific snapshot: Jan–Aug 2026 clothing inflation ranges from about −3.1% in Labuan to +1.7% in Negeri Sembilan.

Supply chain: China and Viet Nam dominate the largest import routes; thicker lines mean higher 2025 apparel and footwear import value.

Concentration: China alone supplies about USD 1.18 billion of the three selected fashion chapters in 2025, far ahead of the next-largest origins.

PRODUCT CATEGORIES

What types of fashion products enter Malaysia?

2025 import composition

Product mix: knitted apparel is the largest import category, while China appears as the biggest supplier block across all three product groups.

Supplier specialisation: Bangladesh is strongly knitted-apparel-heavy, while Viet Nam and Indonesia lean more toward footwear; China dominates overall volume.

Trade asymmetry: China dominates Malaysia’s import side, while the United States is the largest export destination among the major partners shown.

03

SEASONAL TRENDS & CHANGE

When does retail activity rise — and how does the product mix change?

This section directly addresses the assigned topic’s seasonal and time-change focus. The retail series shows the strongest lift at year end, while trade data tracks how knitted apparel, woven apparel and footwear change in relative importance from 2015 to 2025.

December stands out: MSIC 477 sales average about 5.8% above each year’s mean in 2023–2025. Group 477 is a broad specialised-store proxy and includes more than fashion.

Price differences: the heatmap shows how clothing inflation varies by state and year, with 2026 representing the available months through August.

Changing mix: knitted apparel remains the largest import category, but the relative size of all three product groups changes over time. This is a market-availability signal, not a direct survey of individual preferences.

PRICE CONTEXT

How has the price of clothing changed over time?

KEY TAKEAWAY

Across the 16 state and territory series, the clothing-and-footwear index averages about 90 in Aug 2026, while the all-items index averages about 134. In other words, clothing prices have fallen relative to 2010 while the broader basket became much more expensive.

How to read this visualisation

Who

Designed for the average Malaysian reader, with plain-language labels and no assumed statistical background.

What

OpenDOSM retail and clothing-CPI data combined with UN Comtrade HS 61, 62 and 64 data for knitted apparel, woven apparel and footwear.

Why

To explore Fashion and Clothing through product categories, seasonal retail trends and change over time — the core tasks in the assigned Topic 8 brief.

How

Four map idioms show supply and price geography; a treemap and supplier-profile chart compare fashion categories; radial, heatmap, streamgraph and line charts show seasonal and long-term change.
